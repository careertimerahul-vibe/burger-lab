## Content Plan (Post 3x per week)

### Week 1-2: Introduction
- Post 1: "We're Here!" — cart photo, location, hours
- Post 2: Top 3 Bestsellers — photos of Tandoori Paneer, Paneer Paradise, Loaded Fries
- Post 3: Behind the scenes — burger being made, sizzle shots

### Week 3-4: Menu Highlights
- Bestseller close-ups with prices in caption
- Combo deals
- Customer testimonials / reposts

### Ongoing Content Ideas
- New item launches
- "Burger of the day" polls in Stories
- Customer photos (with permission)
- "How we make it" reels (20-30 sec prep videos)
- Sizzle/food ASMR clips
- Limited-time offers
- Late-night craving posts (8PM-11PM posting)
