# Google Review Link — The Burger Lab

## Quick Way (from your phone)
1. Open Google Maps app
2. Search "The Burger Lab Amrapali Golf Homes"
3. Tap your business listing
4. Scroll down and tap "Write a review"
5. Copy the URL from the share button — that's your review link

## From Business Profile Manager (more reliable)
1. Go to https://business.google.com/
2. Select "The Burger Lab"
3. Click "Ask for reviews" in the left menu
4. Copy the link shown — this is your permanent review link

## Once you get it
The link will look like:
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJ...

Share this everywhere:
- WhatsApp status / broadcast
- Instagram bio link
- Printed QR code at the cart
- In your order confirmation messages
