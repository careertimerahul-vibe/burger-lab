## ── BUSINESS DESCRIPTION (750 char max) ──

The Burger Lab is Greater Noida's go-to spot for gourmet veg burgers, grilled sandwiches, and loaded fries — crafted fresh every evening at Amrapali Golf Homes. 🍔🧪

We take classic street food and give it a premium twist: from our bestselling Paneer Paradise and Tandoori Paneer burgers to our signature Fully Loaded Fries, every item is made to order with quality ingredients.

What makes us different:
🔥 Flame-grilled patties & house-made spice blends
🧀 Generous cheese on everything
🌶️ Three flavor zones: Savory, Spicy & Creamy
💸 Prices starting at just ₹60 — premium taste, street-food pricing
📦 Combo meals from ₹180

Open daily 5 PM to 12 AM. Walk in, call, or WhatsApp your order. 

Located at The Burger Lab, Near Entry Gate No. 3, Besides Vegetable Shop, Amrapali Golf Homes Market, Sector 4, Greater Noida.

A: Our top 3 bestsellers are the Tandoori Paneer Burger (₹140), Paneer Paradise Burger (₹100), and Fully Loaded Fries (₹130). The Double Beast Burger (₹120) is also a crowd favorite. Check our full menu on the website.

A: Our entire menu is 100% vegetarian! We specialize in veg burgers, sandwiches, and sides. For Jain (no onion/garlic) requests, please mention when ordering and we'll customize.

Q: Is parking available?
A: Yes! Amrapali Golf Homes market has ample parking space. We're at Shop C-29 — easy to find, right in the main market area.

Q: Do you deliver or take online orders?
A: Currently we operate as a walk-in and pickup cart. You can WhatsApp your order at 9205491224 for quick pickup. We're working on Swiggy/Zomato delivery — stay tuned!

Q: What are your timings?
A: We're open every single day, 5 PM to 12 AM. Late-night burger cravings? We've got you covered.

Q: Do you cater for parties or bulk orders?
A: Yes! For party/bulk orders (10+ items), WhatsApp us at 9205491224 at least 2 hours in advance and we'll have everything ready. Combo deals available for bulk.
